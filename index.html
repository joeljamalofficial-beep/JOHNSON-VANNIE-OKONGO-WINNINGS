Sample Message

FAKE

Congratulations Mr. [REDACTED] your successful win of UGX 1,943,410 🎉. Your winning has already been confirmed and secured under your name.

As per the international betting regulations, every winning above UGX 1,000,000 requires a Releasing Fee of UGX 186,200 before funds can be transferred to your account. This is a standard procedure put in place to ensure smooth processing and avoid any delays or restrictions.

📌 Once the UGX 186,200 Releasing Fee is cleared, your total winning of UGX 1,943,410 will be automatically sent to your MTN account without further delays.

I appreciate your patience and trust throughout this process. This is the final step, and once completed, you will enjoy your full earnings instantly.

Would you like me to provide you with the official payment details now so you can clear the Releasing Fee and receive your winnings?

Scam Anatomy

  • Hook: Unexpected congratulations
  • False authority: “international betting regulations”
  • Upfront fee: “Releasing Fee”
  • Payment rail: MTN Mobile Money
  • Urgency: “final step”, “instantly”
  • Isolation: “official payment details now”
